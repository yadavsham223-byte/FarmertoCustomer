About Us

Farmer To Customer (F2C) is an online platform that connects farmers directly with customers. Our goal is to eliminate middlemen, ensure fair prices for farmers, and provide customers with fresh vegetables, fruits, grains, dairy, and organic products at affordable prices.

Our mission is to support Indian farmers through digital technology while making healthy farm products easily available to every household.
